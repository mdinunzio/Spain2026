Splitting your time between Deià and Santanyí is the absolute smartest way to do Mallorca. It gives you the two best, contrasting sides of the island: Deià provides the dramatic, cliffside, "Ravello" mountain vibe, while Santanyí gives you access to the flatter southeast, which has the pristine, white-sand, Caribbean-clear coves.

Because late August/early September is still peak season for luxury travelers, the absolute best spots will sell out months in advance. Here is your curated list for both regions, with the **"Book Immediately"** items clearly flagged.

### Part 1: Deià & The Tramuntana Coast (The "Ravello" Vibe)

**Hotels**
*   **Belmond La Residencia:** *(BOOK IMMEDIATELY)* This is the gold standard of the island. It is exactly what you are looking for—romantic, deeply authentic but ultra-luxurious, with an incredible art collection and impeccable service. Their rooms for late August will disappear quickly.
*   **Hotel Corazón:** If the Belmond is full, look at this newly opened, highly curated luxury boutique hotel between Deià and Sóller. It has a slightly more bohemian, design-forward luxury vibe.

**Restaurants**
*   **Ca's Patro March:** *(BOOK IMMEDIATELY)* A rustic, open-air seafood restaurant built directly into the cliffside above Cala Deià. It’s famous (it was heavily featured in *The Night Manager*) and reservations are notoriously difficult to get. You want a late lunch here after swimming in the cove.
*   **El Olivo:** *(BOOK IMMEDIATELY)* Located at the Belmond. You want to book a table on the terrace right at sunset. It is arguably the most romantic dining room on the island.
*   **Nama:** Located right in the village of Deià. It serves incredible Asian-Mediterranean fusion with a stunning outdoor terrace overlooking the valley. Great for a night when you want a break from traditional Spanish food.

**Beach Clubs**
*   *A quick note on Deià:* The Tramuntana coast is rugged. You won't find plush, Miami-style beach clubs here; you find wild, rocky coves (calas). For a true luxury beach club with plush daybeds and table service on this side of the island, you would need to drive toward Palma to **Purobeach Illetas**.

**Activities**
*   **Private Sunset Sail from Port de Sóller:** *(BOOK IMMEDIATELY)* Skip the crowded group catamarans. Charter a private classic sailboat out of Port de Sóller (about 20 mins from Deià) to sail the dramatic northern coastline at sunset with local wine and tapas.

### Part 2: Santanyí & The Southeast (Pristine Coves & Authentic Villages)

**Hotels**
*   **Can Ferrereta:** *(BOOK IMMEDIATELY)* Located right in the heart of Santanyí village. It’s a meticulously restored 17th-century mansion that feels like a private luxury estate. It has a stunning courtyard pool, incredible design, and puts you walking distance from local boutiques.
*   **Cal Reiet:** A boutique holistic retreat just outside Santanyí. It’s incredibly peaceful, set in lush gardens, and feels very exclusive.

**Restaurants**
*   **VORO:** *(BOOK IMMEDIATELY)* Located a bit further east in Canyamel, but absolutely worth the drive. It holds 2 Michelin stars and offers a brilliant, theatrical tasting menu. Book a private driver for this night.
*   **Restaurant Laudat:** Located in Santanyí. It has a beautiful, romantic courtyard and an exceptional wine list. Perfect for a high-end, relaxed dinner where you can just walk back to Can Ferrereta afterward.
*   **Cassai:** Located in the nearby village of Ses Salines. It has a gorgeous, rustic-chic interior courtyard and serves excellent Mediterranean food. 

**Beach Clubs**
*   **Mhares Sea Club:** *(BOOK IMMEDIATELY)* Located in Llucmajor (a great stop on your way from Deià to Santanyí). It is carved into a former marine quarry with a massive pool overlooking the sea. It’s adults-only, highly refined, and you must reserve a front-row "Bali bed" well in advance.
*   **Cassai Beach House:** Located in Colònia de Sant Jordi. It has a chic, nautical vibe right on the water. Great for a long, rosé-fueled lunch.

**Activities**
*   **Charter a Traditional "Llaut":** The southeast is famous for its stunning coves (like Caló des Moro and Cala Llombards). Because they are beautiful, they get crowded. The luxury "hack" is to charter a *Llaut* (a traditional Mallorcan wooden boat) with a captain to take you cove-hopping by water. You get to swim in the turquoise water without fighting for towel space on the sand.
*   **Santanyí Market (Wednesdays & Saturdays):** One of the best markets on the island. It takes over the whole village. It’s perfect for the "impromptu shopping and exploring" you loved in Ravello—great local ceramics, linens, and artisan foods.

### The "En Route" Experiences (Do these on your transfer day between Deià and Santanyí)

*   **Cooking Class with Soqueta Experiences:** *(BOOK IMMEDIATELY)* Located in Palma. This is your "Mamma Agata" equivalent. It’s a private, highly curated experience that starts with a tour of the Mercat de l'Olivar to buy ingredients, followed by cooking in a gorgeous private kitchen.
*   **Wine Tasting in Binissalem:** The drive from Deià to Santanyí takes you right past Mallorca's premier wine region. Book a private tasting and vineyard tour at **Bodega Ribas** (the oldest winery on the island, stunning heritage estate) or **Tianna Negre** (modern, architectural, excellent bold reds).

I would lock in your flights to determine your exact dates, and then immediately reach out to **Belmond La Residencia** and **Can Ferrereta** to secure your rooms. Once the hotels are locked, we can map out exactly which days to book Ca's Patro March, El Olivo, and the boat charters!
