## Car-free logistics (the post's main value)
- Bus from Barcelona ~2 h; no car needed.
- 15-minute bus between Palafrugell and Begur.
- Palafrugell 1-3 nights (walk to Calella de Palafrugell + El Golfet);
  Begur 2-4 nights as the main base.
- Link beaches on foot via the Camí de Ronda.

## Venues (no new names — all corroborate earlier batches)
