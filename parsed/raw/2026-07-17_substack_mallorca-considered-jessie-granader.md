4 days in Mallorca, based in Deià at La Residencia.

---

Last summer I spent 4 days in Mallorca with my mom and sister — sun-filled,
relaxing, special. Clear blue water, stone villages, mountainous backdrop.

**Hotel — La Residencia (Deià).** "Every inch of La Residencia was truly
breathtaking." A mix of old historical feel while modern; the pool at the heart
of the hotel. Situated in the heart of Deià, "a popular village in Mallorca that
looks like it's out of a post-card." Arrived 6am first day, explored the town
before the pool. Rest of the day at the pool reading, then massages (their cure
to jet-lag) which included **111Skin face masks**. "The town is quite small but
has some really amazing restaurants and boutiques."

**De Monio (Deià)** — "We loved De Monio, a shop & restaurant, for coffee &
matcha." [name uncertain — verify]

**Traumata Grill (La Residencia, Deià)** — first night, exhausted, "we had a
casual dinner at Traumata Grill at the hotel, which was incredible." [name
uncertain — verify]

**Boat day — Perfect Charter (Port de Sóller).** "Our second day was one of my
favorites, we charted a boat through Perfect Charter out of the Port of Soller.
They took us around to different beaches and coves that were only accessible by
boat. We could not believe how gorgeous the water was, it was crystal clear and
the perfect temperature." Full day on the boat, 10–5:30.

**Es Vergeret** — "We stopped at Es Vergeret (their suggestion) for lunch, it
was a very local restaurant with amazing views." (Boat-day lunch stop; Es
Vergeret is at Cala Tuent, near Sa Calobra.)

**Sebastian (Deià)** — after the boat day, ice cream at the port, then "We
walked into Deia and had dinner at Sebastian."

**Valldemossa** — "We had heard about the amazing markets, so the next day we
headed into Valledemossa, a small village in between Palma and Deia, in the
morning. The town was adorable, however the market was small (this is not the
main one people go to, that one is in Santanyi which was an hour and a half from
our hotel). Found a stand with unique beautiful shell necklaces."

**Can Costa** — "Dinner that night was at Can Costa."

**Hike — Camí dels Pintors (Deià).** "On our last day we did a panoramic hike,
Camí dels Pintors, which was truly spectacular. We hiked along the coastline and
it truly felt meditative. We started at 10 and ended around 12:45 at Cas Patros
March [Ca's Patró March], one of the most recommended spots. It was so gorgeous
and the fresh seafood with high quality olive oil was delicious." (Also a nod to
Mallorca as a wellness hub — "do this hike.")

**Ca's Patró March (Cala Deià)** — the panoramic hike's endpoint; fresh seafood
with high-quality olive oil. "One of the most recommended spots."

**Nama (Deià)** — "Our last dinner was at Nama in Deia, and let me tell you, it
was the best meal of our trip. The pad thai was life-changing and my Hugo spritz
was crispy and refreshing. It was the perfect ending to our trip."

Closing: "Mallorca was a dream… Staying in Deia was such a highlight and so was
our hotel."
