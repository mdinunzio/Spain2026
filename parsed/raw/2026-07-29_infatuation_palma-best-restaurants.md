## The 18 (in guide order)

1. **Mercat de Santa Catalina** (Santa Catalina) — classic market with metallic counters; Bar Joan Frau (breakfast llonguets, variat) and Arume sushi bar (dim sum with langoustines, oxtail). Standing room, walk-ins.
2. **La Rosa Vermutería** (Sant Jaume) — "worth waiting in line": house vermouth, giant tortilla pintxo, Mallorcan pork sausages, pork cheeks in vermouth, rice with smoked mushrooms.
3. **Stagier** (Santa Catalina) — tiny room from a Chilean-Peruvian couple; chimichurri-butter sourdough, lobster-filled avocado rolls. "The most exciting addition in recent years." Book weeks ahead.
4. **Bar La Sang** (Arxiduc) — natural wine, burrata-anchovy tostadas. **Currently temporarily closed.**
5. **Marc Fosh** (La Missió) — 17th-century monastery; lunch from €69, dinner from €139; watermelon soup with dill-cucumber spheres, caviar on crumpets. "Probably the most famous destination restaurant in Palma."
6. **Bodega La Rambla** (Plaça des Patins) — 1940s tavern; house variat (ensaladilla, fried squid, octopus fritters, cod); vegetarian variat available.
7. **El Perrito** (Santa Catalina) — llonguets with Mahón cheese or cured ham and tomato; "so good they opened a second location."
8. **Toque de Queda** (Sant Jaume) — converted ancient bakery; charcuterie boards, roasted baby potatoes with paprika aioli, burrata with kale pesto; tables on a sunny staircase street.
9. **El Camino** (Sant Nicolau) — Barrafina-team tapas counter; padrón peppers, fried zucchini flowers with goat cheese, individual tortillas.
10. **Bar Espanya** (Sant Nicolau) — pintxos stacked on the counter (limited supply — arrive early); patatas bravas with sobrassada; second location in Sa Gerreria with terrace.
11. **Mercat de l'Olivar** (Mercat) — 1950s food hall; Mercat Negre for fresh seafood small plates, Buades for ibérico and cheeses.
12. **Botànic** (La Llotja) — 16th-century mansion garden (Can Bordoy); salted tomato with Mallorcan-prawn carpaccio, spicy mango gazpacho; candlelit and romantic.
13. **Vandal Palma** (Santa Catalina) — nighttime-only lounge; ceviche–coconut-foam cone, croquettes with huitlacoche; cocktail pairings. "Basically demands you have a great time."
14. **DINS Santi Taura** (La Calatrava) — 11-course Mallorcan heritage tasting menu near the cathedral; razorfish with fried eggs, prawns with sobrassada; chef's table. "Leading the way" in Palma fine dining.
15. **Aromata** (listed Sant Jaume) — suckling pig with ensaïmada in ham-citrus sauce, squid-aioli sandwiches, tumbet; value weekday lunch menus, dinner tasting from €75. *(Note: relocated Oct 2024 to HM Palma Blanc, Carrer de Ramon y Cajal 12 — the guide's mansion framing describes the old Concepció location.)*
16. **Fornet de la Soca** (Sant Nicolau) — heritage bakery reviving recipes from local homes and monasteries; ensaïmades (chocolate, cream, rare plaited potato), coca with roasted peppers, fig cupcakes.
17. **Fera** (Sant Jaume) — restored mansion with cobble-floored orange-and-palm garden; Austrian chef fusing Mallorcan produce with pan-Asian flavors (beets with ponzu, wagyu carpaccio in tom kha gai); vegetarian tasting menus.
18. **La Vieja** (Sa Gerreria) — Canarian: papas arrugadas in volcanic-stone bowls with two mojos (one blended tableside); colorful murals, sunny terrace.
