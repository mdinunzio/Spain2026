# Restaurante Sa Foradada (official site) — sea-view restaurant list

**Source:** restaurantesaforadada.com (provided by Michael)

## "Looking for a Restaurant with sea views?"

The site recommends four fellow seaside restaurants along the coast,
accessible by boat (name + location only; no ownership connection stated):

- Es Vergeret (Cala Tuent)
- Es Port (Port de Valldemossa)
- Ca'n Lluc (Cala Deià)
- Ca's Patró March (Cala Deià)

## Sa Foradada details from the site

- Open in 2026. Rice and paella cooked over wood, local fish and seafood,
  salads, local desserts; paella is the must-order.
- **Access:** primarily by boat from Port de Sóller (~45 min), disembark at
  the small pier and climb rustic stairs. Foot access is a ~1-hour hike
  from Son Marroig — the site does not recommend it.
- **Booking:** review guidance says you have to book — "call early morning
  as they're preparing the food." (Corrects the earlier 'first come,
  first served' intel from the Manus booking list.)
