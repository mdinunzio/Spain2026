# Palma Restaurants — Traditional Cooking with a View

Base: Palma Riad (old town). All spots below are walkable or a 10–15 minute cab. Criteria: traditional Mallorcan/Mediterranean cooking, good vibes, outdoor views, no tasting menus.

**Trip dates:** Aug 30 – Sep 11. Early September is still busy — book terraces a few days ahead.

---

## Ca n'Eduardo — Moll de Pescadors
- **Getting there:** 15-min walk from the old town along the seafront
- **What it is:** 80-year-old seafood institution sitting on top of the fish auction at the fishing pier
- **Order:** Lobster paella, whatever fish came up from the market below
- **View:** Terrace across the harbor to the cathedral
- **Notes:** Kitchen runs continuously 1pm–10pm, so it works for an early dinner too
- **Web:** caneduardo.com

## Sa Roqueta — Portixol
- **Getting there:** 25-min seafront walk east of the cathedral, or a short cab
- **What it is:** Family-run since 1987 in an old fisherman's house in Palma's "village" neighborhood
- **Order:** Fish paella, aguiat de peix (fish stew), grilled fish
- **View:** The small Portixol harbor
- **Notes:** Easy dinner night without leaving the city

## El Bungalow — Ciutat Jardí
- **Getting there:** ~10-min cab from the center
- **What it is:** 1920s beach house at sea level on the rocks, family-run for decades, beloved by locals
- **Order:** Seafood paella, salt-baked sea bass, sherry clams
- **View:** Straight across the bay to the cathedral; sunset dinner is the move
- **Notes:** Book the terrace. The building has been in a long-running dispute with the coastal authority — confirm it's open when you book

## Mesón Ca'n Pedro — Génova
- **Getting there:** ~10-min cab up to the hillside village above Palma
- **What it is:** Since 1976, the classic locals' Sunday-lunch spot for Mallorcan mountain cooking
- **Order:** Pa amb oli, frito mallorquín, snails, lamb or fish cooked on a hot stone
- **View:** Summer terrace over the whole bay of Palma
- **Notes:** Different vibe from the seafront places — this is the "drive to Alaró" experience without the drive
- **Web:** canpedro.es · Tel: 971 70 21 62

## Sa Cranca — Passeig Marítim
- **Getting there:** 10-min walk from the old town past the marina
- **What it is:** Palma's long-running paella and rice specialist
- **Order:** Any of the rice dishes or fideuà
- **View:** Marina and yachts in front, cathedral lit up behind at night
- **Notes:** Low-effort night when you want rice done properly

---

## If you only do two
1. **El Bungalow** — sunset dinner, best view-to-price ratio near the city
2. **Mesón Ca'n Pedro** — mountain-style Mallorcan food with the bay below

## Deliberately skipped
Béns d'Avall, Es Fum, El Olivo, Tramuntana Grill — beautiful, but tasting-menu / white-tablecloth territory.

Further afield (need a car, 30–60 min): Ca's Patró March (Cala Deià), Sa Foradada, Es Verger (Alaró), Es Vergeret (Cala Tuent), Es Canyis (Port de Sóller), Mirador de Cabrera, Es Grau (Estellencs).
