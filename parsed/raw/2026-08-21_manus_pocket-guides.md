# Manus — Pocket Guides: Spontaneous Activities & Walk-ins (Deià / Palma / Llafranc)

**Source:** Manus conversation (three pocket-guide deep dives)
**URL:** https://manus.im/app/ab5BWyMlDZtFqjZJo9zm5e
**Captured:** 2026-08-21
**Purpose:** no-advance-booking, decide-on-a-whim options for each leg's
base: Es Molí/La Residencia (Deià), the Palma Riad (Old Town), and Hotel
Mas Pastora (Llafranc).

## Deià pocket guide
Walk-ins: Sa Fonda (village heart; tapas, burgers, live music after 9 PM) ·
Balm ("traveling grandma's kitchen," veg-forward, family-run, very highly
rated) · de moniö (tiny vegetarian cafe, natural wines) · Hotel Corazón
restaurant (open to non-guests; farm-to-table terrace lunch, short drive).
Shopping: Caserra 71 (Matthew Williamson concept store, 3 rooms of a
townhouse) · Obsolete Collective + Island Born (handmade furniture,
fashion; Lost in Beauty wellness lab) · Joy, Made by Hand (straw hats,
alpaca, Mallorcan textiles) · Ceramics by Joanna (since the 90s;
hand-painted local fruits/flora) · Gres Gallery (three generations of
female ceramicists; minimalist vessels).
Active: Painter's Path (Camí dels Pintors) Cala Deià → Llucalcari (~2.5 h
round trip; secret rocky cove, natural mud baths) · Robert Graves' House
(~€7 walk-in, garden) · Sant Joan Baptista church + cemetery (best
panoramic viewpoint over valley and sea, 30-45 min).
Sunsets: Son Marroig (drive/taxi an hour before sunset) · Bar Miró at La
Residencia (live music, cocktails).

## Palma pocket guide (base: Palma Riad, Old Town)
Walk-ins: Bar Espanya · Bodega La Rambla · El Camino · Toque de Queda ·
La Trastienda (terrace under the La Rambla plane trees; classic tapas).
Coffee/brunch: El Perrito · La Molienda · Fornet de la Soca (eat it on
Plaça del Mercat benches) · Mistral Coffee Roasters.
Drinks: Bar La Sang · Brassclub (1950s NY club, glass-bottle ceiling,
€12.50-16.50) · Clandestino Cocktail Club (€10-12) · Singular Rooftop
(Hotel Sant Francesc; romantic sunset) · El Cielo del Born (Nivia Born;
cathedral views, live music) · Sky Bar at Hotel Saratoga (skyline).
Shopping: Passeig del Born + Avinguda Jaume III (luxury/mainstream) ·
Santa Catalina (vintage; Villa Vintage) · Sa Gerreria (artisan quarter) ·
Folklorious · ISSO.
Active: Bellver Castle walk · Paseo Marítimo → Portixol (~30 min) ·
Es Baluard (free terrace) · Es Jonquet windmills (sunset) · Mercat de
l'Olivar.

## Llafranc pocket guide (base: Hotel Mas Pastora)
Walk-ins: Calau (#1 tapas) · La Llagosta (beachfront, steps from hotel;
seafood + rice) · Bar Tres Pins (bay-view tapas + sunset drinks) ·
Sol i Mar · La Croissanteria (waterfront coffee/pastries).
Drinks: Merci Llafranc · El Balcó de Calella (walk via coastal path) ·
El Pati de l'Estrella (10-min taxi; Saturday live music).
Active: Camí de Ronda Llafranc→Calella (25 min flat — "do this multiple
times") · Camí de Ronda Llafranc→Tamariu (~2-2.5 h each way, moderate,
rugged; swim + lunch reward in Tamariu) · Far de Sant Sebastià (30-40 min
uphill).
Sightseeing: Cap Roig Gardens · Begur village boutiques + free castle
360° views · Peratallada & Pals (20 min; preserved medieval stone
villages, artisan shops).
Ideas: beach kayak/SUP rental from Llafranc or Calella sand; hora del
vermut at 12:30 at any beachfront cafe.
