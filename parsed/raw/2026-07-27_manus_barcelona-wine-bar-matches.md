Your favorite: **Barcelona Wine Bar** (Stamford, CT) — rustic Spanish small plates, 400+ wine list, warm/moody atmosphere, grilled meats and peppers, casual-but-elevated vibe. The **Chicken Pimientos** is roasted chicken thigh with potatoes, lemon, and hot cherry peppers.

## Deià

| Restaurant | Why It's Similar | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sa Fonda** | The village's beloved tapas bar — rustic, wine-forward, amazing flavor-packed small plates in a cozy, almost family-like atmosphere. Bustling and energetic in the evenings. | Right in the center of Deià village. Walk-in friendly. This is your "neighborhood spot" during the Deià leg. |
| **Restaurante Deià** | Local white wine, stuffed peppers, squid — very much the rustic, home-cooked Mallorcan vibe with excellent local wines. | Simple, honest food. Not fancy but deeply satisfying. |

## Palma

| Restaurant | Why It's Similar | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Xoriguer** (Santa Catalina) | A beloved Palma institution since the 1980s. Mediterranean-Basque cuisine with grilled meats, an outstanding and reasonably priced wine list, and a warm, rustic atmosphere. The sirloin with Mallorca wine is legendary. | Rated 9.2/10 on TheFork. Bookable on OpenTable. This is probably your single closest match to Barcelona Wine Bar. |
| **Tast Club** (Old Town) | A hidden tapas bar tucked inside a vaulted-ceiling courtyard mansion. Excellent cocktails, carefully curated wine list, and elevated tapas at reasonable prices. Romantic, moody, intimate. | Described as "brimming with romance." Perfect date night. |
| **La Vinya de Santa Clara** (Old Town) | A tiny, owner-run wine bar with 60+ wines by the glass, Spanish charcuterie, cheeses, and simple tapas. The owner personally guides you through the wines. Chilled-out vibe away from the tourist hustle. | Very intimate — feels like discovering a secret. Great for a pre-dinner wine stop or a light evening. |
| **Bar Día** (La Lonja/Born) | A truly rustic local tapas bar — traditional Mallorcan food, grilled padrón peppers, croquettes, tortilla, excellent local wines. Incredibly affordable (€42 for dinner for two with wine). | The most "authentic neighborhood bar" on this list. No frills, just great food and wine. |
| **El Camino** (Old Town) | High-end tapas bar with a long counter and open kitchen, created by the team behind London's Barrafina. Bustling energy, excellent Spanish wines, and beautifully executed small plates. | More polished than Barcelona Wine Bar but the same DNA — counter seating, watching chefs work, sharing plates. |
| **Bar La Sang** (Near La Rambla) | A brilliant natural wine bar serving earthy Mallorcan reds alongside tapas like burrata and anchovies. Intimate, wine-geek-friendly. | Perfect if you love discovering small-producer wines. |

## Costa Brava (Begur Area)

| Restaurant | Why It's Similar | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rostei** (Begur village center) | A family-run restaurant since 1991, housed in an 1849 building with a beautiful wisteria-shaded terrace. Known for grilled meats, grilled clams, and a warm, rustic atmosphere. Ranked #10 in Begur. | This is your "neighborhood grill" in Begur — very much the roasted-chicken-and-wine energy you love. |
| **El Tapas de Begur** (Begur village) | An authentic Spanish tapas bar right in the village center. Simple, quick, friendly — the kind of place you pop into for a glass of wine and a few plates before or after dinner. | More casual/quick-bite than Barcelona Wine Bar, but the same tapas-bar soul. |
| **Vinilo Wine Bar & Bistro** (L'Escala, 25 min drive) | Natural/biodynamic wines, homemade food from local produce, curated vinyl soundtrack, right by the sea. Intimate and artisanal. | If you're willing to drive (or taxi) a bit, this is the most wine-forward option on the Costa Brava. |
| **Entre Dos Mons** (Palamós, 15 min drive) | A Michelin-recognized restaurant with Peruvian-Catalan fusion, an incredible wine list focused on small Empordà producers, and charming hosts. The owner personally introduces you to wines you'd never find elsewhere. | More elevated than Barcelona Wine Bar, but the same passion for wine discovery and bold flavors. |

## Top 3 Picks (If You Only Choose a Few)

1. **Xoriguer** (Palma) — Closest overall match to Barcelona Wine Bar's vibe, wine list depth, and rustic grilled-meat energy.
2. **Tast Club** (Palma) — The most romantic of the bunch, with the same tapas-and-wine-club concept.
3. **Rostei** (Begur) — Your "Barcelona Wine Bar equivalent" in Costa Brava — grilled meats, warm atmosphere, village setting.

## Booking Notes

None of these need to be booked months in advance (unlike Ca's Patro March), but I'd recommend reserving **Xoriguer** and **Tast Club** a week or so ahead for a Friday/Saturday night. The others are walkable, walk-in-friendly neighborhood spots.
