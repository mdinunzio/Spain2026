## Attached document: "Seamless Beach Access in Mallorca: Luxury Alternatives to Santanyí"

You are entirely correct to reconsider Santanyí if your priority is waking up and walking directly onto the sand or into the sea. While Can Ferrereta is a stunning property, it is located inland, meaning you would have to drive 10 to 15 minutes to reach the coast. Since you already have the dramatic mountain and village experience secured with Belmond La Residencia and Es Molí in Deià, balancing that with a property that offers immediate, effortless water access is the perfect strategy.

Finding a luxury hotel in Mallorca that sits directly on the water—without being swallowed by mass tourism or feeling "trashy"—is challenging because the island's coastline is heavily protected or already developed. However, there are a few exceptional properties that cater specifically to your travel style and budget, offering pristine environments, sophisticated service, and direct access to the Mediterranean.

Here are the top alternatives for the second leg of your trip, ranked by how well they fit your criteria.

### 1. Four Seasons Resort Mallorca at Formentor
**The Vibe:** A private Mediterranean estate with unparalleled beach access.

If your goal is to step out of your room and onto one of the most beautiful beaches in the Mediterranean, this is the premier choice. Originally opened in 1929 as the legendary Hotel Formentor, the property was completely rebuilt and reopened by Four Seasons in late 2024. It is located on the Cap de Formentor peninsula in northern Mallorca, surrounded by 100 acres of pine forests and the Tramuntana mountains [1].

The resort offers direct access to Formentor Beach, a kilometer-long stretch of golden sand and calm turquoise water that holds Blue Flag environmental status [2]. While all beaches in Mallorca are public, the resort operates a dedicated concession with private loungers, umbrellas, and full food and beverage service delivered to the sand [2]. Beyond the beach, the property features a working vineyard offering tastings and dinners, multiple dining venues, and extensive wellness programming [1]. It is larger than a boutique hotel, but it provides a self-contained, highly polished luxury experience where you never need to get in a car.

### 2. Pleta de Mar (Canyamel)
**The Vibe:** Sleek, adults-only eco-luxury with exclusive cove access.

Located on the island's east coast near Canyamel, Pleta de Mar is a five-star, adults-only property designed to blend seamlessly into the natural landscape. The architecture is modern and minimalist, utilizing local stone and wood, and the hotel is surrounded by lush gardens and pine trees [3].

The property offers exclusive access to Sa Cova dels Albardans, a secluded pebble beach reached via a private path from the hotel grounds [4]. While it is not a sprawling white-sand beach, it provides immediate, private access to the sea for swimming and snorkeling. The hotel also features two stunning infinity pools with Balinese beds overlooking the Mediterranean [4]. This is an excellent option if you prefer a quieter, design-forward boutique environment over a larger resort.

### 3. Fontsanta Hotel Thermal Spa & Wellness
**The Vibe:** Adults-only wellness retreat near Mallorca's most famous beach.

If you are willing to compromise slightly on *immediate* beach access in exchange for proximity to the island's most pristine stretch of sand, Fontsanta is a remarkable property. Located in the south near Campos, it is a five-star, adults-only hotel built around the only natural thermal water spring in the Balearic Islands [5].

While the hotel is situated three kilometers inland, it provides a complimentary, exclusive shuttle service directly to Es Trenc [6]. Es Trenc is widely considered Mallorca's most beautiful beach—a protected natural park featuring miles of untouched white sand and Caribbean-clear water, entirely free of large resort developments [7]. Fontsanta offers a deeply peaceful, wellness-focused atmosphere that contrasts beautifully with the rugged energy of Deià.

### A Note on Cap Rocat
You may come across Cap Rocat in your research, as it is one of the most famous luxury hotels in Mallorca. It is a spectacular 19th-century military fortress converted into a 30-room, adults-only hotel overlooking the Bay of Palma [8]. It features a private swimming lagoon accessed via a staircase carved into the cliffs [9].

However, I advise caution. Recent reviews from high-end travelers indicate that while the property and service are extraordinary, the rooms—which are built into the subterranean fortress—can be dark, damp, and in need of renovation [8]. Unless you can confirm that the rooms have been recently updated, the Four Seasons or Pleta de Mar will offer a more comfortable and seamless experience for this leg of your trip.

### Recommendation
I strongly suggest looking into the **Four Seasons Resort Mallorca at Formentor** first. It provides the exact contrast you need after Deià: effortless access to a stunning sandy beach, comprehensive luxury amenities, and a deeply historic, natural setting that avoids the crowded tourist zones entirely.
