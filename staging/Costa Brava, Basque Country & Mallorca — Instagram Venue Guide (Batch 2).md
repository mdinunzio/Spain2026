## Detailed Post-by-Post Breakdown

### 1. NU Beach Club, Mallorca
**Source:** [instagram.com/reel/DK95gxRsFj_/](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DK95gxRsFj_/) · **Creator:** @pockettraveldeals (Adrian & Katie)

A showcase of **NU Mallorca**, called "one of Mallorca's most beautiful beach clubs." Located on **Playa de Muro** (northeast Mallorca), it features wooden cabanas draped in sheer fabric directly on the sand, clear turquoise water with mountain views, a shaded outdoor dining terrace, an indoor bar with woven wicker pendant lights, and a lounge with white chairs and pink cushions. Food shown includes guacamole, salsa, hummus, and tortilla chips. The creators highlight great food and drink options and **a DJ on site throughout the day**. Cost not stated. **Rating: 5 (inferred)** — presented as a must-visit.

### 2. San Juan de Gaztelugatxe, Basque Country
**Source:** [instagram.com/reel/DYcrj2-N8w5/](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYcrj2-N8w5/) · **Creator:** @elina.outdoor.explorer (Elina Aleksandra)

"One of the most unreal hikes in the Basque Country" — the islet of **San Juan de Gaztelugatxe** near **Bermeo**, famous as the *Game of Thrones* Dragonstone filming location. The hike (~20 minutes each way) crosses a stone bridge over the ocean and climbs hundreds of cliff-carved stairs to a tiny hermitage. **Practical tips:** access is free, but the site is a protected area requiring an **online reservation in advance**; the creator got in without one by repeatedly refreshing the booking page for cancelled slots. **Rating: 5 (inferred)** — described as breathtaking and unreal.

### 8-9. Via Ferrata Cala del Molí (two creators)
**Sources:** [instagram.com/reel/C6q1pvwNRdy/](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C6q1pvwNRdy/) · @soranachiper (Sorana Chiper) and [instagram.com/reel/DZK6Xs2vV0_/](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZK6Xs2vV0_/) · @kimmconn (Kimmie Conner)

Two independent reels covering the **Via Ferrata Cala del Molí** in **Sant Feliu de Guíxols** — "the only via ferrata by the sea in Europe." Sorana's reel (Romanian) gives the specs: **~8 km route, 20 m height, difficulty K1-K2 (easy), 1-1.5 hours**, featuring Nepalese rope bridges over the waves; accessible for beginners yet "super fun" for experienced climbers. Kimmie calls it "one of the most underrated adventure activities I've ever done... equal parts beautiful and terrifying" and rates it explicitly: **"10/10 would happily spend my holiday dangling off cliffs again."** The route itself is public and free; helmet/harness/lanyard rental or guided tours cost extra (not stated). **Rating: 5 (explicit)** — the strongest multi-creator consensus in this batch.

## Cross-Batch Observations

The strongest signals in this batch come from **multi-creator consensus**: **Cap Sa Sal** in Begur appears in three posts (one creator's "fav restaurant ever"), the **Aiguablava / Sa Tuna / Illa Roja / Platja Fonda** beach cluster around Begur appears in three separate posts each, and the **Via Ferrata Cala del Molí** is raved about by two independent creators (one explicit 10/10). Begur clearly emerges as the epicenter of Costa Brava recommendations, playing the same role Deià/Sóller played in the Mallorca batch.

Only two concrete prices surfaced on screen: **€28.50/100 g wagyu sirloin at Komo To Loko** and the note that **Bar Rodondo is "cheap & good."** Free-but-regulated access applies to **San Juan de Gaztelugatxe** (mandatory online reservation — book ahead or refresh for cancellations) and the via ferrata (bring or rent proper equipment). Note also that the @travellingcloset itinerary post is a **paid partnership (ad)**, so its uniformly glowing ratings should be weighted accordingly.
