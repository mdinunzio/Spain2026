Yes! Sa Foradada is absolutely accessible by foot from the mainland — that's actually the most popular way to get there. Here's how it works from La Residencia:

---

## Getting to Sa Foradada on Foot from La Residencia

**Step 1: Drive (or taxi) to Son Marroig**
- Son Marroig is the historic estate on the MA-10 coast road, about **10 minutes by car** west of Deià (and La Residencia).
- You cannot walk from La Residencia to Son Marroig comfortably — it's along a winding road with no sidewalk and fast traffic. Drive or have the Belmond arrange a quick transfer.

**Step 2: Hike down from Son Marroig to Sa Foradada**
- The trailhead starts at the Son Marroig estate parking area.
- It's a **45-60 minute hike downhill** to the Sa Foradada peninsula and restaurant.
- The path is well-marked but rocky and steep in sections. Wear proper shoes (not sandals).
- Elevation drop: ~300 meters (about 1,000 feet).

**Step 3: The return hike back up**
- This is the hard part. The **climb back up takes 60-90 minutes** depending on fitness and heat.
- In late August/early September, it will be hot. Bring plenty of water and start early or go in the late afternoon.

---

## The Full Experience

The classic move is:
1. Drive to Son Marroig (~10 min from La Residencia)
2. Hike down (45-60 min)
3. Arrive at the restaurant, order their famous **wood-fired paella** and a cold beer/wine
4. Swim off the rocks in the crystal-clear water
5. Hike back up (60-90 min)
6. Watch sunset from Son Marroig's marble temple at the top (if you time it right)

**Total time commitment:** ~4-5 hours including the meal and swim.

## Feasibility Assessment

| Factor | Rating |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Fitness required** | Moderate. The hike down is easy; the hike back up is a real workout, especially in the heat. If you did the Ravello-to-Amalfi hike last year, you can definitely handle this. |
| **Shoes** | Sneakers or hiking sandals minimum. No flip flops. |
| **Heat** | Late August will be hot (30-35°C). Go early morning (arrive by 10 AM) or late afternoon (start at 4-5 PM for sunset timing). |
| **The restaurant** | No reservation needed — it's first come, first served. But it's popular at lunch, so arriving by 1 PM is smart. |
| **Alternative access** | You can also reach Sa Foradada **by boat** from Port de Sóller. Ask the Belmond concierge about their hotel boat — this would be the luxury option (boat there, hike back up, or boat both ways). |
