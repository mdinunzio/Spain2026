# Deià Pocket Guide: Spontaneous Activities & Walk-ins

This guide is designed for your days at Es Molí and La Residencia. Everything here requires **no advance booking** and can be decided on a whim.

## 🛍️ Shopping & Browsing
Deià isn't a massive shopping hub, but it has excellent, highly curated artisan boutiques perfect for impromptu browsing — all on or just off the main street, an easy loop from either hotel.

**Caserra 71** (🚶 4 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Caserra%2071%2C%20Mallorca%2C%20Spain)) is British designer Matthew Williamson's brand new boutique concept store, set across three rooms of a historic townhouse. You will find vintage finds, beach coverups, party dresses, and objects that mix 1970s design with Mediterranean languor.

**Obsolete Collective (and Island Born)** (🚶 5 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Obsolete%20Collective%2C%20Mallorca%2C%20Spain)) is a multi-room boutique focusing on handmade furniture, fashion, and accessories. They feature beautiful fabrics, natural dyes, and a dedicated "Island Born" room highlighting Balearic brands. They also have a wellness lab (Lost in Beauty) if you want a spontaneous facial or massage.

**Joy, Made by Hand** (🚶 3 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Joy%2C%20Made%20by%20Hand%2C%20Mallorca%2C%20Spain)) is a tiny gem focused on ancestral techniques, offering straw hats from Colombia, Alpaca blankets, and Mallorcan textiles.

**Ceramics by Joanna** (🚶 2 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Ceramics%20by%20Joanna%2C%20Mallorca%2C%20Spain)) has been a local institution since the 90s, run by a Chicago-born artist. The shop is filled with hand-painted ceramics decorated with local fruits and flora—the perfect authentic souvenir.

**Gres Gallery** (🚶 1 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Gres%20Gallery%2C%20Mallorca%2C%20Spain)) provides a peaceful refuge run by three generations of female ceramic artists. The gallery showcases minimalist, ancient-looking ceramic vessels and art pieces.

## 🌅 Drinks & Sunsets

For the best sunset views without a dinner reservation, drive to **Son Marroig** (🚗 8 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Son%20Marroig%2C%20Mallorca%2C%20Spain)). This historic estate features a neoclassical marble temple perched on the cliff edge and is universally considered the best sunset viewpoint on the island. Just drive or taxi over about an hour before sunset.

Alternatively, if you prefer not to leave the hotel, **Bar Miró at La Residencia** (at your hotel · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Caf%C3%A9+Miro+La+Residencia%2C+Dei%C3%A0%2C+Mallorca%2C+Spain)) offers live music, excellent cocktails, and mountain views right from their own terrace.
