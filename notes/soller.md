## How to Book the Ferrocarril de Sóller

**Website:** [trendesoller.com](https://trendesoller.com/eng/train-ticket)

### Online Booking:
- **Online sales are available for the combined round-trip ticket** (Train Palma → Sóller + Tram Sóller → Port de Sóller + return)
- **You can only book up to 7 days in advance** — so you'd book on or after **Saturday August 29th**
- Tickets can only be purchased online **on the day of the trip or up to 7 days before**

### Pricing (2026):
| Ticket Type | Price per Person |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Combined round-trip** (train + tram, Palma → Port de Sóller → Palma) | ~€40 |
| **One-way train** (Palma → Sóller) | ~€23 |

### My Recommendation:
**Buy the combined round-trip ticket online (€40/person)** — it covers the full experience: train there, tram to the port, tram back, train home. It's the simplest option.

## The Schedule (Saturday)

Trains depart from **Palma station** (Plaça d'Espanya — walkable or a short taxi from the Palma Riad) roughly every 30-60 minutes. Typical departure times include:

- 10:10 AM
- 10:50 AM  
- 12:15 PM
- 1:00 PM
- (+ afternoon departures for return)

**The journey takes ~1 hour** through the Tramuntana mountains — it's genuinely one of the most scenic train rides in Europe.

## Tips

- **Book online the morning of, or up to 7 days before** (so from Aug 29 onward). The 10:10 AM departure is the most popular on Saturdays — book early in the morning to secure it.
- **Saturdays are the busiest day** because of the market. The train can sell out. Don't wait until you're at the station — book online the night before or first thing that morning.
- **Bring cash** for the market stalls (many don't take cards).
- **The train station in Palma** is at Plaça d'Espanya — about a 10-15 minute walk from the Old Town / Palma Riad area.
