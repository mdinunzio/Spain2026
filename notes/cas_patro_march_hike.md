Hiking down to Cala Deià is a fantastic idea and completely transforms the dinner into a full Mediterranean experience.

You can absolutely go for a swim beforehand, provided you plan your attire strategically. Here is exactly what to expect and how to pull it off without risking your table.

### The Hike Details

* **The Route:** The trail from the village is a 2.9-mile round trip loop with approximately 770 feet of elevation gain.
* **The Terrain:** The path is famously rocky with sections of loose gravel, so you must wear sturdy shoes with good grip rather than sandals for the actual walk down.

### The Swim & The Dress Code

* Ca's Patró March is perched directly on the rocks above the water, meaning they are entirely accustomed to guests arriving from the cove.
* However, while the daytime atmosphere is very relaxed, the restaurant recommends "smart-casual" attire for dinner.
* You will not lose your reservation for having wet, salty hair from the ocean, but sitting at a table in a dripping wet swimsuit or a sweaty athletic shirt will not fly.

### The Execution Strategy

* **Pack Smart:** Bring a small day pack for the hike.
* **The Quick Change:** Pack a towel and a dry, elevated outfit to change into after your swim. A fresh rag & bone t-shirt or a crisp button-down paired with your Lululemon Commission pants will easily meet the smart-casual requirement while keeping you comfortable. Chelsea can simply slip a polished sundress over her swimsuit.
* **Timing:** Aim to arrive at the cove about an hour before your reservation. This gives you plenty of time to swim, dry off in the late afternoon sun, and change out of your wet gear before walking up to the restaurant.
