# Thursday, September 10 in Llafranc
## A Choose-Your-Energy Menu from Hotel Mas Pastora

**Prepared for Michael and Chelsea**  
**Base:** Hotel Mas Pastora, Llafranc ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Hotel%20Mas%20Pastora%2C%20Llafranc%2C%20Spain))  
**Goal:** Preserve a genuinely restful final day while keeping several worthwhile activities available if you become restless.

## The short answer

Do **not** formally schedule this day. Your location makes it unusually easy to decide after breakfast. Hotel Mas Pastora is approximately a five-minute, mostly flat walk from the Llafranc waterfront and **Tourist Service** ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/C%2F%20Cypsela%201%2C%2017211%20Llafranc%2C%20Girona%2C%20Spain) · [🌐 Website](https://www.touristservice.es/en/home-2-english/) · 🚶 6 min), the local kayak, paddleboard, taxi-boat and skippered-boat operator.[1] The three strongest spontaneous pivots are therefore:

| How you feel | Best plan | Commitment |
|---|---|---:|
| **We want to do almost nothing** | Llafranc beach, long lunch and hotel pool | None |
| **We want one fun activity** | Walk down and rent a double kayak or two SUPs | 1–2 hours |
| **We want scenery and movement** | Camí de Ronda to Calella or Sant Sebastià lighthouse | 1–3 hours |
| **We want a real adventure** | Guided kayak-and-snorkel excursion | Approximately 2.5 hours |
| **We want to explore** | Begur old town and castle | Approximately 3 hours |
| **We want a proper half-day outing** | Pals plus Peratallada | Approximately 4–5 hours |

## Energy level 0: deliberately do nothing

### Llafranc beach and promenade

> [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Platja%20de%20Llafranc%2C%20Girona%2C%20Spain) · 🚶 ~7 min from the hotel

This is the correct default. Walk down after breakfast, rent loungers if available, swim, have lunch on the promenade and return to the hotel whenever you wish. The beach is sandy rather than a rock platform, and the waterfront is close enough that you can retreat to the pool or room without making the outing feel like a commitment.

### Add the easy Calella walk only if inspired

The Camí de Ronda ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Cam%C3%AD%20de%20Ronda%2C%20Llafranc%2C%20Spain)) between Llafranc and Calella de Palafrugell ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Calella%20de%20Palafrugell%2C%20Girona%2C%20Spain) · 🚶 ~24 min / 🚗 13 min) is the gentlest possible activity upgrade. It follows the coast to Calella’s small coves and waterfront in roughly 20–30 minutes each way at an unhurried pace. Stop for coffee, gelato or lunch and turn around whenever you have had enough. The official coastal network follows the GR-92’s red-and-white markings.[2]

**Reservation:** None.  
**Transport:** None.  
**Best timing:** Morning swim, late lunch, hotel pool afterward.

## Energy level 1: spontaneous water activity

### Kayak rental from Llafranc beach

Tourist Service rents stable single and double sea kayaks directly on the waterfront. Current published rates are:

| Rental | Single kayak | Double kayak |
|---|---:|---:|
| **1 hour** | €20 | €30 |
| **2 hours** | €35 | €50 |
| **4 hours** | €45 | €70 |

A **double kayak for two hours** is the best unplanned option: enough time to paddle close to Llafranc’s rocky shoreline and stop for a swim without turning the day into an expedition.[3]

### Stand-up paddleboard

SUP rentals are €20 for one hour, €35 for two hours and €45 for four hours. The operator suggests paddling south toward Calella and El Golfet ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Platja%20del%20Golfet%2C%20Calella%20de%20Palafrugell%2C%20Spain)) or north toward openings in the cliffs, but SUP range is naturally shorter than kayak range.[4]

**Reservation:** Walk-up may be possible, but call after breakfast to protect against limited September staffing or sea conditions.  
**Contact:** Tourist Service, +34 696 02 72 03; info@touristservice.es. · [🌐 touristservice.es](https://www.touristservice.es/en/home-2-english/) · [📍 C/ Cypsela 1, 17211 Llafranc](https://www.google.com/maps/search/C%2F%20Cypsela%201%2C%2017211%20Llafranc%2C%20Girona%2C%20Spain)  
**Best timing:** Before noon, when the sea is generally the most comfortable.  
**Bring:** Swimsuit, water, sun protection, towel and shoes/sandals that can get wet.

## Energy level 2: guided kayak plus snorkeling

This is the **best adventure-to-effort ratio**. Tourist Service’s guided excursion costs €50 per person and lasts approximately 2.5 hours. It includes the kayak, guide, snorkeling equipment and underwater photographs. Depending on weather and the group, possible routes include El Golfet, Sant Sebastià, Cala Pedrosa, Tobacco Cave and other narrow coves or passages inaccessible by larger craft.[5]

This is materially better than renting a kayak independently if you want caves, more ambitious coastline and a proper snorkel stop without navigating unfamiliar water.

**Reservation:** Contact one or two days beforehand, but ask for a cancellable/weather-dependent place so the day remains flexible.  
**Best timing:** Morning.  
**Who should choose it:** Both comfortable swimming and spending 2.5 active hours on the water.

## Energy level 2: short private boat without sacrificing the day

Tourist Service also runs private skippered coastal outings. For two to five guests, published prices are €175 for one hour, €275 for two hours, €375 for three hours and €475 for four hours. Routes can include caves, coves, Les Formigues ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Illes%20Formigues%2C%20Girona%2C%20Spain)), Tamariu/Cap de Begur or the Palamós side, with swimming and snorkeling stops.[6]

A **two-hour private boat for €275** is an excellent spontaneous luxury pivot: enough to see the coast from the water without repeating the full-day Palma charter experience.

**Reservation:** Call ahead; not a reliable walk-up.  
**Best use:** Request caves plus one swim/snorkel stop rather than extensive cruising.

## Energy level 2: scenic hikes

### Sant Sebastià lighthouse and archaeological site

> [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Far%20de%20Sant%20Sebasti%C3%A0%2C%20Llafranc%2C%20Spain) · 🚶 26 min uphill / 🚗 8 min

Walk north from Llafranc and climb to Cap de Sant Sebastià for broad coastal views, the lighthouse complex, chapel and Iberian archaeological remains. Allow roughly 45–60 minutes uphill from the waterfront depending on pace, then linger at the viewpoint. This is the best compact workout.

**Return:** Walk down or take a short taxi if the heat or your legs argue against the descent.  
**Shoes:** Sneakers or walking shoes.

### Llafranc to Cala Pedrosa or Tamariu

> Cala Pedrosa: [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Cala%20Pedrosa%2C%20Tamariu%2C%20Girona%2C%20Spain) (foot or boat only, ~45–60 min on the path) · Tamariu: [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Platja%20de%20Tamariu%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain) (🚶 2–2.5 h via the path / 🚗 9 min)

This is the real hike. The Llafranc–Tamariu Camí de Ronda section is approximately 6.6 km one way, is commonly completed in 2–2.5 hours and includes rocky trail, elevation change and secluded Cala Pedrosa.[7]

The elegant version is to hike **one way** and return by pre-arranged taxi or Tourist Service’s taxi boat. Do not casually start a round trip late in the day; it would no longer be a chill-day activity.

## Energy level 1–2: gardens, art and a cloudy-day pivot

### Cap Roig Botanical Gardens

> [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Jardins%20de%20Cap%20Roig%2C%20Calella%20de%20Palafrugell%2C%20Spain) · 🚗 17 min

Cap Roig is a 20-hectare Mediterranean garden with approximately 800 botanical species, contemporary sculpture, a castle and coastal viewpoints. A complete self-guided circuit takes approximately 1.5 hours. On September weekdays it is scheduled to open from 10:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., with last entry one hour before closing; general admission is €7.[8]

This is the best refined, low-exertion excursion and pairs naturally with Calella or El Golfet.

### Can Mario and the Cork Museum, Palafrugell

> [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Museu%20Can%20Mario%2C%20Palafrugell%2C%20Spain) · 🚗 10 min

For cloudy weather, Can Mario is a contemporary Catalan sculpture museum in a former cork factory, displaying approximately 220 works plus outdoor sculpture. On Thursday, September 10 it falls within the museum’s summer schedule, Wednesday through Sunday from 11:00 a.m. to 7:30 p.m.; admission is €10.[9] The Cork Museum and modernist water tower are in the same complex, with the tower providing views over Palafrugell and Empordanet.[10]

**Drive/taxi:** Approximately ten minutes from Llafranc.  
**Best use:** Two-hour cultural break followed by lunch or shopping in Palafrugell.

## Energy level 2: Begur village excursion

Begur ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Begur%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain) · 🚗 16 min) is the easiest inland village pivot, approximately **16 minutes by car** from Mas Pastora.[11] Its compact historic center combines the ruined medieval castle, 16th-century defense towers, Gothic church and numerous 19th-century “Indian” houses built by residents who returned after making fortunes in the Americas.[12]

The castle walk is short but uphill, with expansive views. The lanes below are appropriate for browsing boutiques, cafés and wine shops. This is the best choice if you want something resembling the spontaneous village exploring you enjoyed in Ravello.

**Reservation:** None.  
**Transport:** Use your rental car during the day. Parking is easier on the edge of the historic center than in its narrow core.  
**Allow:** Three hours including coffee/drinks and the castle.

## Energy level 3: Pals plus Peratallada

If you wake up wanting a genuine outing, pair these medieval villages. Google Maps currently estimates about **27 minutes total driving from Mas Pastora through Pals to Peratallada**, excluding stops.[13]

In **Pals** ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Pals%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain) · 🚗 18 min direct), follow the historic route through Plaça Major, the medieval walls, Torre de les Hores, Sant Pere church and Josep Pla viewpoint. In **Peratallada** ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Peratallada%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain) · 🚗 26 min direct), wander the rock-cut lanes, Plaça de les Voltes, defensive towers and castle-palace. Both are beautifully preserved, but Pals offers stronger views while Peratallada has the more immersive stone-village atmosphere.[14]

**Reservation:** None for the villages; reserve lunch only if targeting a specific restaurant.  
**Transport:** Rental car.  
**Allow:** Four to five hours with lunch.  
**Trade-off:** This consumes most of the chill day, so choose it only if both of you actively want to explore.

Jet skiing is **not** the best ad hoc choice. It requires confirmation, travel and a fixed slot, while kayaking or a short boat from Llafranc delivers caves and swimming with much less friction. If Chelsea strongly wants jet skis, reserve ahead; otherwise leave them off the plan.

## Energy level 3: beginner scuba

Triton Diving ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Triton%20Diving%2C%20Llafranc%2C%20Spain) · 🚶 2 min) is based in Llafranc and has operated since 1979. Its site displays 4.8/5 ratings from both Google and Tripadvisor and publishes boat-based try-dive experiences around €105 per person in its 2026 calendar.[17]

This is a legitimate adventure, but not something to improvise casually after breakfast. Confirm the exact September 10 schedule, medical requirements and total duration beforehand.

## Three ready-made versions of the day

### Plan A — Stay restful

Spend the morning at Llafranc beach. Walk to Calella for lunch, browse the coves, then return to Mas Pastora for the pool and an early evening drink. Nothing needs booking.

### Plan B — Restful with one memorable activity

Call Tourist Service after breakfast. Rent a double kayak for two hours or join the guided kayak-and-snorkel trip if space exists. Have a long waterfront lunch and spend the afternoon at the hotel pool.

### Plan C — We woke up restless

Drive to Begur, walk to the castle and browse the old town. Return toward Llafranc for a late beach lunch and swim. This supplies a meaningful excursion without sacrificing the whole day.

## My recommendation

Preserve September 10 as unstructured, but save **Tourist Service (+34 696 02 72 03 · [🌐 Website](https://www.touristservice.es/en/home-2-english/))** in your phone. On the morning itself, choose between beach-only, a two-hour double kayak or the guided kayak-and-snorkel outing according to weather and energy. If the sea is unappealing, drive 16 minutes to Begur. That gives you four excellent day shapes without pre-committing your final day.
