# Chiringuitos & Beach-Casual — Llafranc Base (Sep 7–11)

Quick-lunch and drinks-on-the-beach options from Hotel Mas Pastora, nearest first. No reservations needed anywhere except Toc al Mar. **September note:** true chiringuitos run into mid/late September but shorten weekday hours; carry some cash for the rustic ones.

## 🚶 At your door — Llafranc beach (4–8 min)
- **La Llagosta** — seafood + rice right on the front; the default walk-up lunch. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/La%20Llagosta%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)
- **Blau Llafranc** — premium tapas on the promenade: garlic gambas, fried boquerones. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Blau%20Llafranc%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)
- Sunbed/kiosk service on the sand for a no-shoes drink; **La Croissanteria** for the morning coffee run. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/La%20Croissanteria%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)

## 🚶 Calella de Palafrugell (19–25 min via Camí de Ronda · 🚗 13 min)
- **Bar Tres Pins** — the closest true chiringuito vibe: casual tapas + sunset drinks overlooking the bay. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Bar%20Tres%20Pins%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)
- **La Vela** — waterfront salads, mussels, casual paella; pure walk-in. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Restaurante%20La%20Vela%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)
- **Calau** — grab-what-looks-good pinchos + cava under the voltes. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Calau%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)
- **Sol i Mar** — crispy squid and socarrat paella at local prices. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Sol%20i%20Mar%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)

## 🥾 Cala Pedrosa — the adventure one (on the Llafranc→Tamariu hike, ~45–60 min; also boat-accessible)
- **The fishermen's-hut chiringuito** ⭐ — an old fisherman's hut on a pebble cove reachable only by the coastal path (~800 m steep spur) or by sea: omelettes, pa amb tomàquet, day-boat fish, and rice dishes cooked in the open air by the woman who runs it. Fair prices, cash, zero pretension — the single most "only here" lunch of the leg. Natural stop on the Tamariu hike or an anchor-drop on the Nautiq boat day (Sep 9). [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Cala%20Pedrosa%2C%20Tamariu%2C%20Girona%2C%20Spain)

## 🚗 Tamariu (9 min)
- **Bar Rodondo** — "cheap & good" on the Passeig del Mar, plus beachfront terraces along the sand. The reward end of the big hike. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Bar%20Rodondo%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)

## 🚗 The Begur coves (15–18 min)
- **Aiguablava — Toc al Mar** — the famous oak-fire beach restaurant ON the sand; the one entry here that needs a reservation (your 5-rated flagship lunch — book, don't drop by). A small drinks kiosk serves the beach itself. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Toc%20al%20Mar%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)
- **Cala Aiguafreda — the chiringuito** — unnamed beach bar over the quietest rocky cove; Hostal Sa Rascassa's garden is steps away if a drink becomes dinner. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Cala%20Aiguafreda%2C%20Begur%2C%20Spain)
- **Sa Tuna — Es Furió** (starred pick: "try the arroz mar") and **Restaurante Sa Tuna**, both on the tiny promenade — chiringuito energy, proper kitchens. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Es%20Furi%C3%B3%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)
- **Sa Riera & Platja de Pals** — several chiringuitos along the long sand. ⚠️ The famous **Illa Roja** seasonal bar is a July–August operation — don't count on it your week. [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Platja%20de%20Sa%20Riera%2C%20Begur%2C%20Spain)

## Rule of thumb
Walking the Camí de Ronda? You're never more than ~25 minutes from a cold drink: Llafranc front → Calella (Tres Pins) → El Golfet (no service — bring water) → or north to Pedrosa's hut → Tamariu (Rodondo).
