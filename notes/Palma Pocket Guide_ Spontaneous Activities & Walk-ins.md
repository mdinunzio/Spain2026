# Palma Pocket Guide: Spontaneous Activities & Walk-ins

This guide is designed for your days based at the Palma Riad in the Old Town. Everything here is highly vetted, requires **no advance booking**, and can be decided on a whim.

## 🍽️ Casual Bites & Walk-in Dining
When you want excellent food without the formality of a reservation. Palma's tapas scene is world-class.

| Restaurant | The Vibe | The Food | When to Go |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bar Espanya** | A classic pinchos bar tucked behind Plaça Major. It is almost always bustling and full of energy. | Freshly made pinchos and traditional tapas like patatas bravas with sobrassada. | Perfect for a mid-day snack or a casual, lively dinner. Just walk in and find a spot at the bar. |
| **Bodega La Rambla** | A beloved Palma institution since the 1940s located at the north end of La Rambla. | Famous for their house *variat* (a large plate combining multiple tapas like fried squid, octopus fritters, and ensaladilla). | Great for a sit-down lunch or standing at the bar for quick snacks. |
| **El Camino** | A stylish, long bar centered around an open kitchen in the Sant Nicolau area. | Sizzling Padrón peppers, delicate fried zucchini flowers, and local Mallorcan wines. | Expect a line, but the wait is worth it. Ideal for an early dinner or late lunch. |
| **Toque de Queda** | An unfussy Mallorcan-Italian deli and small-plates spot in a converted ancient bakery just off La Rambla. | Exceptional charcuterie boards loaded with cheeses, grapes, and jams, plus Mediterranean tapas. | Grab an outdoor table on the street staircase for a sunny afternoon snack with cava. |
| **La Trastienda** | A comfortable terrace set under the tall plane trees of La Rambla. | Solid, classic tapas in a relaxed outdoor setting. | When you want to sit outside and people-watch over a few small plates. |

## ☕ Coffee, Brunch & Cafes
Palma has an incredible specialty coffee and brunch scene.

**El Perrito** in Santa Catalina is widely considered to have the best brunch in Palma. It is the perfect place to try a classic *llonguet* (a Mallorcan bread roll stuffed with Mahón cheese or cured ham). **La Molienda** in the Old Town is loved by both locals and tourists for its excellent breakfast and specialty coffee. 

For a more historic experience, **Fornet de la Soca** on Plaça Weyler is a must-visit. There is always a crowd gazing into the window at their traditional baked goods, including sugar-topped ensaïmades and crisp coca pastry. Grab a pastry and eat it on the benches in nearby Plaça del Mercat. If you are serious about your coffee, **Mistral Coffee Roasters** is arguably the most sought-after specialty coffee brand on the island.

## 🍸 Evening Drinks: Cocktails, Wine & Rooftops
Palma comes alive in the evening. All of these are walkable from the Palma Riad.

| Venue | Category | The Experience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bar La Sang** | Natural Wine | A cool, laid-back natural wine bar about 20 minutes from the Old Town. Excellent tostadas with burrata and anchovies alongside earthy Mallorcan reds. |
| **Brassclub** | Cocktails | Classy 1950s New York club vibes with a stunning glass bottle ceiling. The mixologists here are masters, serving highly creative drinks (€12.50-16.50). |
| **Clandestino Cocktail Club** | Cocktails | A quirky, cozy hidden gem on a quiet side street. Relaxed atmosphere with excellent signature drinks and new prohibitions (€10-12). |
| **Singular Rooftop Terrace** | Rooftop | Located atop the Hotel Sant Francesc in the heart of the Old Town. Undeniably romantic, perfect for sunset cocktails and Mediterranean snacks. |
| **El Cielo del Born** | Rooftop | At the Nivia Born Boutique Hotel overlooking Paseo del Born. Fantastic views of the Cathedral and city skyline, often with live music. |
| **Sky Bar at Hotel Saratoga** | Rooftop | Offers a front-row seat to Palma's iconic skyline, from the harbor boats to the Cathedral. |

## 🛍️ Shopping & Browsing
Palma offers everything from luxury houses to bohemian concept stores.

**Passeig del Born (Paseo del Borne)** is Palma's most elegant, tree-lined boulevard, home to luxury brands like Louis Vuitton and Loewe. Just off the Born is **Avenida Jaume III**, the main shopping artery mixing international brands with local boutiques.

For independent designers and concept stores, head to the **Santa Catalina** neighborhood, where you will find vintage shops like Villa Vintage. The **Sa Gerreria** area is also emerging as an artisan quarter. **Folklorious** and **ISSO** are excellent design-forward concept stores worth browsing in the center.

## 🥾 Active, Scenic & Cultural (No planning required)
When you want to explore the city beyond the restaurants.

| Activity | What It Is | Why Do It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bellver Castle Walk** | A unique 14th-century circular castle on a hilltop surrounded by pine forest. | The walk up through the forest takes 30-40 minutes and rewards you with panoramic 360° views of Palma, the bay, and the mountains. |
| **Paseo Marítimo to Portixol** | A flat, scenic waterfront promenade walk heading east along the bay. | After about 30 minutes, you reach Portixol, a charming former fishing village now packed with trendy cafes and a small beach. |
| **Es Baluard Museum** | Palma's contemporary art museum built directly into the old city walls. | Excellent modern art, plus a massive terrace with sweeping views over the harbor (free to access the terrace). |
| **Es Jonquet Windmills** | A short walk from Santa Catalina to the historic windmill quarter. | It provides a fantastic, elevated sunset viewpoint over the harbor and a glimpse into Palma's agricultural past. |
| **Mercat de l'Olivar** | Palma's main indoor market. | A sensory overload of Balearic cheeses, garlic strings, and fresh seafood. Grab a glass of wine and some ibérico ham at one of the stands. |
