# Palma Pocket Guide: Spontaneous Activities & Walk-ins

This guide is designed for your days based at the Palma Riad in the Old Town. Everything here is highly vetted, requires **no advance booking**, and can be decided on a whim.

> 🧭 Times are measured from the **Palma Riad** (Carrer de Sant Jaume). Almost everything is walkable — driving in the Old Town is slower than walking; where a taxi genuinely helps, both times are shown. Tap 📍 for Google Maps.

## ☕ Coffee, Brunch & Cafes
Palma has an incredible specialty coffee and brunch scene.

**El Perrito** (🚶 13 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/El%20Perrito%2C%20Palma%2C%20Mallorca%2C%20Spain)) in Santa Catalina is widely considered to have the best brunch in Palma. It is the perfect place to try a classic *llonguet* (a Mallorcan bread roll stuffed with Mahón cheese or cured ham). **La Molienda** (🚶 8 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/La+Molienda%2C+Mallorca%2C+Spain)) in the Old Town is loved by both locals and tourists for its excellent breakfast and specialty coffee.

For a more historic experience, **Fornet de la Soca** (🚶 4 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Fornet%20de%20la%20Soca%2C%20Palma%2C%20Mallorca%2C%20Spain)) on Plaça Weyler is a must-visit. There is always a crowd gazing into the window at their traditional baked goods, including sugar-topped ensaïmades and crisp coca pastry. Grab a pastry and eat it on the benches in nearby Plaça del Mercat. If you are serious about your coffee, **Mistral Coffee Roasters** (🚶 4 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Mistral+Coffee+House%2C+Mallorca%2C+Spain)) is arguably the most sought-after specialty coffee brand on the island.

## 🛍️ Shopping & Browsing
Palma offers everything from luxury houses to bohemian concept stores.

**Passeig del Born (Paseo del Borne)** (🚶 3 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Passeig%20del%20Born%2C%20Mallorca%2C%20Spain)) is Palma's most elegant, tree-lined boulevard, home to luxury brands like Louis Vuitton and Loewe. Just off the Born is **Avenida Jaume III**, the main shopping artery mixing international brands with local boutiques.

For independent designers and concept stores, head to the **Santa Catalina** neighborhood (🚶 15 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Santa%20Catalina%20Market%2C%20Palma%2C%20Mallorca%2C%20Spain) to the market at its heart), where you will find vintage shops like Villa Vintage. The **Sa Gerreria** area is also emerging as an artisan quarter. **Folklorious** and **ISSO** are excellent design-forward concept stores worth browsing in the center.

## 🥾 Active, Scenic & Cultural (No planning required)
When you want to explore the city beyond the restaurants.

| Activity | From Hotel | What It Is | Why Do It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bellver Castle Walk** | 🚶 49 min · 🚗 13 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Bellver+Castle%2C+Mallorca%2C+Spain) | A unique 14th-century circular castle on a hilltop surrounded by pine forest. | The walk up through the forest takes 30-40 minutes and rewards you with panoramic 360° views of Palma, the bay, and the mountains. |
| **Paseo Marítimo to Portixol** | 🚶 44 min · 🚗 11 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Portixol%2C+Mallorca%2C+Spain) | A flat, scenic waterfront promenade walk heading east along the bay. | After about 30 minutes on the promenade itself, you reach Portixol, a charming former fishing village now packed with trendy cafes and a small beach. |
| **Es Baluard Museum** | 🚶 10 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Es+Baluard+Museum+of+Modern+Art%2C+Mallorca%2C+Spain) | Palma's contemporary art museum built directly into the old city walls. | Excellent modern art, plus a massive terrace with sweeping views over the harbor (free to access the terrace). |
| **Es Jonquet Windmills** | 🚶 19 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Es+Jonquet+Windmills%2C+Mallorca%2C+Spain) | A short walk from Santa Catalina to the historic windmill quarter. | It provides a fantastic, elevated sunset viewpoint over the harbor and a glimpse into Palma's agricultural past. |
| **Mercat de l'Olivar** | 🚶 12 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Mercat%20de%20l%27Olivar%2C%20Palma%2C%20Mallorca%2C%20Spain) | Palma's main indoor market. | A sensory overload of Balearic cheeses, garlic strings, and fresh seafood. Grab a glass of wine and some ibérico ham at one of the stands. |
