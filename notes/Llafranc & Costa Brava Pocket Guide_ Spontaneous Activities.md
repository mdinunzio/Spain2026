## 🍸 Evening Drinks & Sunsets
When you want a cocktail or a glass of wine with a view.

**Merci Llafranc** (🚶 4 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Merci%20Llafranc%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)) is a vintage-style cocktail bar right in your village, often featuring live music. It is the perfect spot for a post-dinner drink without needing a taxi.

**El Balcó de Calella** (🚶 32 min · 🚗 14 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/El%20Balc%C3%B3%20de%20Calella%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)) is arguably the best sunset spot in the immediate area. It sits on a cliff in Calella de Palafrugell, offering panoramic views of the Mediterranean. Walk over from Llafranc via the coastal path.

**El Pati de l'Estrella** (🚗 11 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/El%20Pati%20de%20l%27Estrella%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)) in Palafrugell town is a bohemian 1950s vermouth bar. If you want to experience the local *hora del vermut* (vermouth hour) or catch live music on a Saturday, this is the place.

## 🛍️ Shopping & Sightseeing
When you want to browse or take a short drive to explore.

**Cap Roig Botanical Gardens** (🚗 17 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Cap%20Roig%20Botanical%20Gardens%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)) are located just south of Calella de Palafrugell. This stunning clifftop garden features over 800 botanical species and a sculpture park overlooking the sea. It is a peaceful, beautiful way to spend a morning.

**Begur Village** (🚗 16 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Begur%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)) offers the best boutique shopping in the area. The town is filled with independent clothing boutiques, ceramics, and artisan shops. While there, walk up to the **Begur Castle ruins** for free 360° views of the coastline.

**Peratallada** (🚗 26 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Peratallada%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)) **& Pals** (🚗 18 min · [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Pals%2C%20Costa%20Brava%2C%20Spain)) are two of the most perfectly preserved medieval stone villages in Spain. They are incredibly photogenic, with cobblestone streets, ivy-draped archways, and small artisan shops. Perfect for a spontaneous late-afternoon excursion.

## 🌊 Other Spontaneous Ideas
- **Rent a Kayak or SUP:** Walk down to the Llafranc or Calella beachfront and rent a kayak or stand-up paddleboard for an hour. Paddle along the cliffs to discover tiny, hidden sea caves you cannot reach on foot.
- **Hora del Vermut:** Adopt the local Catalan tradition. At 12:30 PM, sit at any beachfront cafe in Llafranc, order a glass of local vermouth on ice with an olive, and share a plate of anchovies or chips before lunch.
