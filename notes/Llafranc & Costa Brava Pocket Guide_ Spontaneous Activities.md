# Llafranc & Costa Brava Pocket Guide: Spontaneous Activities

This guide is designed for your days based at Hotel Mas Pastora in Llafranc. Everything here requires **no advance booking** and can be decided on a whim.

## 🍽️ Casual Bites & Walk-in Dining
The Costa Brava is famous for its unpretentious, high-quality seafood and tapas.

| Restaurant | Location | The Vibe & Food |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Calau** | Calella de Palafrugell | The undisputed #1 tapas spot in the area. A bustling, casual pinchos bar where you just grab what looks good. Perfect for a spontaneous lunch or light dinner. |
| **La Llagosta** | Llafranc | Beachfront dining just steps from your hotel. Excellent fresh seafood and rice dishes with views of the bay. |
| **Bar Tres Pins** | Calella de Palafrugell | A casual spot with a magnificent setting overlooking the bay. Known for fabulous tapas and sunset drinks. |
| **Sol i Mar** | Calella de Palafrugell | A local gem famous for its crispy squid and paella with excellent *socarrat* (the crispy rice at the bottom of the pan). |
| **La Croissanteria** | Llafranc | Your go-to spot for morning coffee, fresh pastries, and light bites right on the Llafranc waterfront. |

## 🍸 Evening Drinks & Sunsets
When you want a cocktail or a glass of wine with a view.

**Merci Llafranc** is a vintage-style cocktail bar right in your village, often featuring live music. It is the perfect spot for a post-dinner drink without needing a taxi.

**El Balcó de Calella** is arguably the best sunset spot in the immediate area. It sits on a cliff in Calella de Palafrugell, offering panoramic views of the Mediterranean. Walk over from Llafranc via the coastal path.

**El Pati de l'Estrella** in Palafrugell town (a 10-minute taxi ride) is a bohemian 1950s vermouth bar. If you want to experience the local *hora del vermut* (vermouth hour) or catch live music on a Saturday, this is the place.

## 🥾 Active & Scenic
The coastline here is defined by the historic Camí de Ronda walking paths.

| Activity | What It Is | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Camí de Ronda: Llafranc to Calella** | The classic coastal walk connecting the two villages. | A flat, paved, 25-minute walk along the cliffs. Stunningly beautiful and effortless. Do this multiple times. |
| **Camí de Ronda: Llafranc to Tamariu** | A longer, more rugged hike north along the coast. | ~2-2.5 hours each way. Moderate difficulty with some elevation. The reward is arriving in the tiny, picturesque cove of Tamariu for a swim and lunch. |
| **Far de Sant Sebastià Hike** | A steep walk up to the historic lighthouse towering above Llafranc. | Takes 30-40 minutes uphill. The panoramic views from the top are the best on this stretch of coast. |

## 🛍️ Shopping & Sightseeing
When you want to browse or take a short drive to explore.

**Cap Roig Botanical Gardens** are located just south of Calella de Palafrugell. This stunning clifftop garden features over 800 botanical species and a sculpture park overlooking the sea. It is a peaceful, beautiful way to spend a morning.

**Begur Village** (a 15-minute drive) offers the best boutique shopping in the area. The town is filled with independent clothing boutiques, ceramics, and artisan shops. While there, walk up to the **Begur Castle ruins** for free 360° views of the coastline.

**Peratallada & Pals** (20 minutes by car) are two of the most perfectly preserved medieval stone villages in Spain. They are incredibly photogenic, with cobblestone streets, ivy-draped archways, and small artisan shops. Perfect for a spontaneous late-afternoon excursion.

## 🌊 Other Spontaneous Ideas
- **Rent a Kayak or SUP:** Walk down to the Llafranc or Calella beachfront and rent a kayak or stand-up paddleboard for an hour. Paddle along the cliffs to discover tiny, hidden sea caves you cannot reach on foot.
- **Hora del Vermut:** Adopt the local Catalan tradition. At 12:30 PM, sit at any beachfront cafe in Llafranc, order a glass of local vermouth on ice with an olive, and share a plate of anchovies or chips before lunch.
