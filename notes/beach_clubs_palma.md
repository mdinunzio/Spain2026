Great list! Here's my breakdown based on your style, budget, and the fact that you're staying at the Palma Riad (which is in the Old Town/center of Palma):

---

## My Top Pick: Purobeach Illetas

**Why it wins for you:**

| Factor | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Vibe** | The plush, Miami-meets-Mediterranean aesthetic. Sleek white daybeds, infinity pool overlooking the sea, DJ soundtrack, excellent cocktails. It's upscale without being pretentious. |
| **Distance from Palma Riad** | ~15 min by taxi (Illetas is just southwest of Palma). Easy there and back. |
| **Beach access** | Yes — it's built into the cliffs above the sea with direct water access via steps down to the rocks/platforms for swimming. |
| **Food & drink** | Full restaurant with sushi, Mediterranean dishes, and a strong cocktail menu. You can easily spend the whole day here. |
| **Price** | $$$ — Daybed reservations typically €100-200 for a couple depending on the day, with a minimum spend that goes toward food/drinks. |
| **The feel** | Exactly what you're looking for: luxurious, scenic, relaxing, Instagram-worthy but not trashy. Think La Fontelina energy but more modern. |

---

## Runner-Up: Balneario Illetas

**Why it's worth considering:**

| Factor | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Vibe** | A massive, gastronomy-focused beach club. More "grand Mediterranean terrace" than "sleek pool club." |
| **Distance from Palma Riad** | Same area as Purobeach — ~15 min taxi. |
| **Differentiator** | The food is reportedly exceptional ($$$$) — it's as much a restaurant as a beach club. If you want a long, luxurious lunch by the sea with wine, this edges out Purobeach on the culinary front. |
| **Price** | $$$$ — more expensive overall. |

---

## The Others — Quick Takes

| Club | My Take |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Gran Folies** (Port d'Andratx) | Beautiful cliffside setting, but it's 35-40 min from Palma. Too far for a casual day when you have Purobeach 15 min away. Better as a day trip activity if you were staying in Andratx. |
| **Assaona** (Portixol) | On the water in Portixol (10 min from Palma). More casual/local, less "beach club" and more "waterfront bar with sunbeds." Fine but not a wow experience. |
| **Mhares Sea Club** (east coast) | Adults-only, ultra-luxurious, stunning. BUT it's in Llucmajor — **45+ min from Palma.** Too far for your Palma-based stay. This would've been perfect if you were staying in the southeast. |
| **Cassai Beach House** (Colònia de Sant Jordi) | Chic and nautical, but it's on the **south coast — 50+ min from Palma.** Same issue as Mhares. |
| **NU Beach Club** (Playa de Muro) | North coast — **1 hour from Palma.** Way too far. |

---

## The Verdict

**Book Purobeach Illetas.** It's the perfect combination of proximity (15 min taxi from your hotel), luxury level, and vibe. You can taxi there mid-morning, spend the day on a daybed with cocktails and swimming, then taxi back to the Palma Riad by 5-6 PM to shower and walk to dinner.

Reserve a **Balinese bed** (the premium daybed option with curtains and shade) for the best experience. Book online at [purobeach.com](https://purobeach.com) or call them directly.

If you want to do a *second* beach club day during your trip and are feeling adventurous, Gran Folies in Port d'Andratx could work as a half-day excursion combined with exploring the harbor town — but only if you have a free day with nothing else planned.
