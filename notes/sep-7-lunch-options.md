# Monday Sep 7 — Lunch Stop, BCN Airport → Llafranc

Land 11:05, driving by ~12:15–12:30, lunch 1:30–2:00. Hotel Mas Pastora check-in 3:00.

**Key catch: it's a Monday.** Half the region's best kitchens close Mondays. Palamós stays fully open; Peratallada and Girona are weakened.

**Route note:** Take the C-31/C-66 exit toward Palafrugell rather than letting GPS route you through La Bisbal's town center at lunch hour.

---

## Option 1: Palamós — the fishing port (RECOMMENDED)

> [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Port%20de%20Palamos%2C%20Girona%2C%20Spain) · 🚗 BCN Airport → Palamós **93 min** · Palamós → Mas Pastora **22 min**

15 min before Llafranc, near-zero detour. The town is famous for gambas de Palamós, red prawns landed at this exact dock. You arrive right as lunch service opens, so no reservation stress.

| Restaurant | Why | Monday hours | Phone |
|---|---|---|---|
| **La Gamba** ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Restaurant%20La%20Gamba%2C%20Palamos%2C%20Girona%2C%20Spain)) | The classic since 1918, on the port facing the boats. Order the *small* gambas a la plancha — tastier than the big ones. | 1:00–3:45 PM | +34 972 31 46 33 |
| **Can Blau** ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Restaurant%20Can%20Blau%2C%20Palamos%2C%20Girona%2C%20Spain)) | One street back; locals' pick for rice dishes and gambas, highest rated. Whole fish is priced by weight — ask before they cook it. | 1:00–4:00 PM | +34 972 31 61 13 |
| **Ca L'Amadeu** ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Ca%20l%20Amadeu%2C%20Marina%20Palamos%2C%20Girona%2C%20Spain)) | In the marina, terrace over the boats, easiest parking (secure lot) — the low-effort option with luggage in the car. | Open through 5:00 PM | +34 972 31 37 43 |

**After lunch:** walk the port past the fishing boats, peek at the Museu de la Pesca ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Museu%20de%20la%20Pesca%2C%20Palamos%2C%20Girona%2C%20Spain)), then the Camí de Ronda to **Cala S'Alguer** ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Cala%20s%20Alguer%2C%20Palamos%2C%20Girona%2C%20Spain)) (whitewashed fishermen's huts, ~25 min each way). Rolls you into Mas Pastora right at 3:00.

## Option 2: Peratallada — the medieval village

> [📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Peratallada%2C%20Girona%2C%20Spain) · 🚗 BCN Airport → Peratallada **104 min** · Peratallada → Mas Pastora **26 min**

10 min inland from the hotel. Honey-colored stone, moat carved into rock, 30–40 min of lanes to wander.

| Restaurant | Why | Monday status | Phone |
|---|---|---|---|
| **El Borinot** ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/El%20Borinot%2C%20Peratallada%2C%20Girona%2C%20Spain)) | Fideuà on a terrace under the medieval arches. Genuinely good. | Open, 12:30–3:15 PM | +34 872 99 37 48 |
| **D.O. Candelaria** ([📍 Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Candelaria%2C%20Carrer%20Major%209%2C%20Peratallada%2C%20Spain)) | The best kitchen in the village by some distance. | **CLOSED Mondays** | +34 635 30 39 11 |

**Better plan:** save Peratallada for **Tuesday Sep 8** after the Mas Oller tasting, when Candelaria is open for lunch (1:00–3:30). Check the 11 AM tasting ends in time for a 1:00 table.

> ⚠️ **Schedule conflict to verify:** the Aug 26 live-calendar check (docandelaria.myrestoo.net) showed Candelaria **closed Tuesday, open Wednesday** — the opposite of this note. Check the calendar before anchoring Tuesday lunch here; El Borinot or El Pedró in Pals are the safe Tuesday fallbacks.

## Option 3: Girona — only if you want a city
