> I'm booking a flight from BCN to PMI on Monday, September 7th, so that we can continue our leg of our trip in Costa Breva after switching out of Palma. How early do you need to get to the airport before a flight, given that it's only 50 minutes, to ensure we get through everything okay?

Flight length doesn't change anything here — a 50-minute hop has the same check-in, security, and gate-closing cutoffs as a long-haul. What matters is that it's a domestic Spanish flight (no passport control) but through two of the busiest airports in Europe in a month that's still high season.

Quick check on direction: you said you're switching out of Palma to continue to Costa Brava, which would be PMI → BCN, not BCN → PMI. Worth double-checking the booking before you pay.

For the leg out of Palma, plan on:

- **90 minutes before departure** if you're carry-on only. PMI security in late summer routinely runs 20–40 minutes in the morning peak, and Monday is a heavy day with weekend traffic heading home.
- **2 hours** if you're checking bags. Vueling and Ryanair close bag drop 40–45 minutes before departure, and the queues at PMI can eat most of that margin.

Hard cutoffs to know: gates typically close 20–30 minutes before departure on both carriers, and neither will hold the plane. Check in online the day before so you already have boarding passes and can skip the desk entirely with carry-on.

Two things that shave real time at PMI: the airport sells a Fast Lane security pass for a few euros that's worth it on a summer Monday, and the terminal is big, so budget 10 minutes to walk to Module C/D gates where most domestic flights leave. On the BCN side, arrival is quick — you're out of Terminal 1 and into a taxi or the train within 15–20 minutes of landing, and it's about 1.5–2 hours by car to the Costa Brava from there.
